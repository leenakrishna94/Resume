Job Title: Sr. IT Governance Analyst for AI
Employer: Randstad Digital
Location: Durham, North Carolina

Summary:
Resource engaged with the AI COE team to collaborate on AI guidelines, standards, and internal development. Provides subject-matter expertise and counsel to leaders and business areas on IT controls, audits, governance, and risk management.

Responsibilities:
- Improve, document, and implement IT software development lifecycle processes and controls.
- Oversee and coordinate the IT audit process with business areas and external firms.
- Ensure effective audit communication and management.
- Identify systemic weaknesses in IT controls and guide resolution actions.
- Escalate issues as needed to obtain resources for remediation.
- Create, implement, and manage processes, policies, procedures, standards, and controls for IT risk management.
- Lead highly complex IT process-change initiatives with critical dependencies and multi-division impact.
- Manage and prioritize the overall portfolio of change initiatives.
- Collect inputs to define future change initiatives.

Required qualifications:
- 5+ years of governance, risk, and compliance experience.
- At least one year performing these functions for AI.
- NIST experience preferred.

Skills:
AI, IT controls, IT software development, effective communication, audit process, audits, governance, IT risk management, process improvement, risk management.
